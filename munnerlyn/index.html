How it's calculated

Spherical equivalent (SE)
SE = Sphere + Cylinder ÷ 2
Ablation depth (Munnerlyn's formula)
Depth (µm) = |SE| (D) × Optical zone² (mm) ÷ 3
Percent tissue altered (PTA)
PTA = (Flap + Ablation depth) ÷ Pachymetry × 100
Residual corneal thickness
Residual = Pachymetry − Flap − Ablation depth
Maximum correctable SE
The largest myopic correction that keeps residual thickness ≥ 300 µm and PTA < 40%: Max SE = 3 × min(Pachymetry − Flap − 300, 0.4 × Pachymetry − Flap) ÷ Optical zone²
