How it's scored

Each risk factor earns 0–4 points, and the total sets the risk category. Your patient's values are highlighted.

Risk categories

  • Low risk (0–2 points): Low likelihood of corneal ectasia. LASIK may proceed with standard precautions.
  • Moderate risk (3 points): Intermediate likelihood of corneal ectasia. Additional evaluation and alternative surgical options should be considered.
  • High risk (4+ points): Significant likelihood of corneal ectasia. LASIK is generally not recommended.

RSB is calculated as CCT − flap − ablation depth (Munnerlyn: |SE| × optical zone² ÷ 3) unless you enter a measured value. The score was derived from patients 18 and older, and it does not include percent tissue altered (PTA): ectasia can occur in eyes with low scores, so also check PTA with the Munnerlyn calculator.

Randleman JB, et al. Risk assessment for ectasia after corneal refractive surgery. Ophthalmology. 2008;115:37–50. For clinical decision support only; verify results and use clinical judgment.

Corneal Topography Options

  • Normal / symmetric bowtie (0 points): Includes round, oval, or symmetric bowtie patterns.
  • Asymmetric bowtie (1 point):
    • Asymmetric steepening in any direction less than 1.0 diopter (D).
    • No skewed radial axis.
  • Inferior steepening / skewed radial axis (3 points):
    • Significant skewed radial axis with or without inferior steepening.
    • One diopter or more of inferior steepening in some areas but an I-S value of less than 1.4.
  • Abnormal (4 points): Includes keratoconus, pellucid marginal corneal degeneration, or forme fruste keratoconus with an I-S value of 1.4 or more.
